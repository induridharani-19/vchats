# VChats Enterprise Release Changelog

---

## [2.0.0] - Enterprise WhatsApp Suite Update

### Added
- **Interactive Polls**: Live multi-choice voting in single & group chats.
- **View Once Media**: Self-destructing binary media sharing.
- **Starred Messages Drawer**: Global bookmarking drawer for essential messages.
- **V-Pay Wallet & Peer-to-Peer Payments**: Built-in simulated crypto/currency transfers inside chat threads.
- **Meta AI Assistant Suite**: AI writing helper, automatic message translation, thread summary generator, and meeting notes extractor.
- **Business Profiles & Product Catalog**: Showcase business hours, catalogs, and quick replies.
- **Chat Export**: Download full chat transcripts formatted as `.txt` files.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts Cheatsheet**: Accessible via `Ctrl+K` for fast navigation.
- **Storage breakdown & Cache Cleaner**: Visual storage gauge bar and instant cleanup.
- **Full 10-Document Enterprise Documentation Suite**: `WHATSAPP_FEATURES.md`, `API_DOCUMENTATION.md`, `DATABASE_SCHEMA.md`, `SOCKET_EVENTS.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `DEPLOYMENT.md`, `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `FEATURE_STATUS.md`, `TESTING_GUIDE.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`.

### Improved
- WebRTC peer connection synchronization & camera stream lifecycle.
- Redux slice state isolation for call streams and active conversation caches.
- Glassmorphic UI theme styling with high contrast and font scaling controls.
